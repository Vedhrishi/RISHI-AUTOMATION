## How to schedule all 10 in about 15 minutes
1. Instagram app → **+** → **Reel** → select the video.
2. Add the cover, then paste the caption from above.
3. Tap **More options → Schedule this post** and set the date and time from the table.
4. Repeat for all 10. Check them under **Profile → ☰ → Scheduled content**.

(On a computer, **Meta Business Suite** → Planner does the same thing.)

## Make every Reel look sharp
1. **Upload in HD:** Instagram → Settings → **Media quality** → turn ON **"Upload at highest quality"**. Without it, Instagram compresses Reels heavily.
2. **Burned-in captions are already in the video**, placed above Instagram's bottom text and away from the right-side buttons, so nothing gets covered. You can still add Instagram's auto-captions for accessibility.
3. **Cover:** pick a frame with Rishi's face and a big title (the first 3 seconds of every Part 1 or Part 2).
4. **Hook first:** the first line of each caption is the hook, because only about 125 characters show before "…more".
5. **Max 5 hashtags.** Every caption here already uses exactly 5.
